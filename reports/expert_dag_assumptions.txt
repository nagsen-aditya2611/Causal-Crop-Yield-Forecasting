EXPERT DAG ASSUMPTIONS
================================================================================

1. Latitude influences regional climatic conditions such as average temperature and incoming solar radiation.

2. Harvest year captures temporal variation in climate, agricultural technology, crop varieties and management practices.

3. Climatic variables (temperature, radiation and climatic water balance) directly influence soil moisture dynamics through evaporation, transpiration and water availability.

4. Soil properties (available water capacity, bulk density and drainage class) determine the storage, movement and retention of water within the soil profile.

5. Surface soil moisture is the primary treatment variable and directly affects crop water availability.

6. Surface soil moisture influences vegetation condition, which is represented by NDVI.

7. NDVI influences FPAR because healthier vegetation intercepts and absorbs more photosynthetically active radiation.

8. Vegetation health (NDVI and FPAR) directly influences crop productivity and final yield.

9. Temperature may directly influence crop yield through heat stress and crop development, independent of soil moisture.

10. Climatic Water Balance directly influences crop yield because prolonged water deficit reduces productivity even after accounting for soil moisture.

11. Crop Area Percentage is used as a proxy for agricultural intensity and management practices that may influence crop productivity.

12. The Expert DAG is constructed using agronomic knowledge and published literature rather than purely statistical associations.
